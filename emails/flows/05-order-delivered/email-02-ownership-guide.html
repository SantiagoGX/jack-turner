Caring for Your Watch
 
To ensure longevity:
01

Wind regularly

Wind automatic models periodically to maintain accuracy.
02

Avoid magnets

Avoid extreme magnetic exposure to protect the movement.
03

Store with care

Store properly when not worn to preserve the finish.
